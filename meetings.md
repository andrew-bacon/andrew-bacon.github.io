# Booking meetings

Current students can [book an appointment here](https://calendar.app.google/fTep82BvDjQTsUY78).

To make my life easier, I'd greatly appreciate it if you could follow these general guidelines:

1. If you book the day before the appointment make sure you've emailed me to check it's ok.
2. Don't have more than one appointment booked at a time, to give everyone a chance to sign up.
3. The default is that you are going to send me written work before the meeting to discuss, which you should send me by the end of Thursday. If you just want to discuss an idea, let me know beforehand. 
4. Please don't book a meeting until you've incorporated feedback from our previous meeting.
5. If I'm not advising you in some capacity, please check with me before booking a meeting.
6. To avoid day of cancellations try not to book on the mere aspiration to have something to discuss by Friday :-D.

Thank you!
