# Booking meetings

Current students can [book an appointment here](https://calendar.app.google/fTep82BvDjQTsUY78).

Please read the following guidelines before booking:

1. Please don't book the day before the appointment unless you've emailed me to let me know you'd like a meeting.
2. Please don't have more than one appointment booked at a time, to give everyone a chance to sign up.
3. You should have written work you want to discuss, unless we've agreed beforehand to discuss a project idea.
4. Please don't book a meeting until you've incorporated feedback from our previous meeting.
5. Please avoid same-day cancellations unless it's an emergency. In particular, don't book an appointment on the assumption that you might have something ready to discuss.
6. If I'm not your primary supervisor, please check with me before booking a meeting.

Thank you!
